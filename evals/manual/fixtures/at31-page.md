# Why we stopped writing design docs

*A post from a small product team. This page is test material for think-twice's AT-31.*

For two years, every feature on our team started with a design doc. Last spring we stopped, and after six months
I'm convinced it was the right call.

Our docs took about a week each to write and review. When we looked back at twenty of them, most of the decisions
they recorded had changed once we started building. The docs were out of date within a month, and nobody read them
after launch.

Since we stopped, we start with a 30-minute whiteboard session and a short list of open questions instead. Our lead
time from idea to first release has dropped from seven weeks to four, and nobody on the team has asked to go back.

So my conclusion is simple: design docs cost more than they're worth for teams like ours, and most small teams
should drop them.
